### 1. Executive Architecture Summary
Niccolò Machiavelli AI ($PRINCE) establishes an autonomous, decentralized, post-quantum intelligence protocol engineered for empirical political realism, game-theoretic institutional resilience, republican self-determination, and popular defense against oligarchic domination. Synthesizing both the strategic pragmatism of *The Prince* and the democratic constitutionalism of the *Discourses on Livy*, the protocol translates classical Renaissance statecraft into verifiable, high-performance Web4 decentralized architectures.
